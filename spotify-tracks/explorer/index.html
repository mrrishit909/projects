How genres sound

Each dot is a genre, placed by the median of its tracks' audio features. Pick any two features; colour shows median popularity (each song's most popular release only). Find a genre to highlight it.

popularity 070

Audio features are Spotify's own estimates (0-1, except loudness in dB, tempo in BPM and length in minutes). Genres are how the dataset's author searched Spotify, with 1,000 tracks each, so they are samples, not the whole catalogue.

Data: Spotify Tracks Dataset (Hugging Face, maharshipandya), collected from Spotify's Web API. Only genre medians are published here.
