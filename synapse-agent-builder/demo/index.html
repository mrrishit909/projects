An agent is a graph. Most teams can't see theirs.

This canvas is a working support agent: six steps, two models, a tool and a search. Scroll to watch it handle an e-mail.

SIMULATED PROVIDERS The models, the CRM and the knowledge base are deterministic simulators in this repository. Nothing calls a real model.

Watch one e-mail move through it.

Particles carry each step's output along its edges at real speed. A node glows while it works; the glow's reach is its share of the run's time, and the bar under it is its tokens.

A step failed. You can see where, and what happened next.

Swap the model. Compare. Gate the release.

On 64 labeled e-mails, Nova Pro instead of Atlas Large costs 70.7% less and is 27.8% faster at p95, and classifies slightly better (92.2% vs 90.6%). But its replies drop the order number more often, so the end-to-end pass rate falls from 89.1% to 82.8%, and the release gate blocks it.
