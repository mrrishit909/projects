Mine Ore Optimizer

An invented open-pit copper mine. A barren dyke the drill holes missed and three truck breakdowns hit the shift; the grade estimator, the engine models and the dispatch and blend optimisers respond; the twin compares the new plan with the old on the same randomness; a second person approves it. …
The mine, its orebody, assays, haul log, engine telemetry and plant are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real mine data is used. The twin is the service's own model; the generator then shows what really happened. No outside model or service is called.
