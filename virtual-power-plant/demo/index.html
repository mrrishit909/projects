Virtual Power Plant

An invented aggregator's 10,000 thermostats, EV chargers, home batteries and solar roofs on eight feeders. Availability learned from sixty days of telemetry, a heat-wave forecast, a bid sized to what the fleet will really do, a dispatch plan that respects every feeder through the rebound, a gateway that goes dark mid-event, and settlement on the market's baseline, every number set against the simulation truth. …
The territory, the devices, their owners' behaviour, the weather, the telemetry and the market are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real utility, aggregator, customer or market. The truth is shown only to judge the models, which never see it. No outside model or service is called.
