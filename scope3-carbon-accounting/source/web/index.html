Scope 3 Carbon Accounting

An invented multinational's two million invoice lines, 40,000 shipments, utility bills and supplier disclosures, turned into a Scope 1-3 inventory where every figure traces back to its invoice and factor, with uncertainty bands, a second person on every high-impact change, and scenarios recalculated from every line. …
The company, its suppliers, invoices, shipments, bills, disclosures and the emission-factor catalogue are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real company's data and no official factor dataset is used. "Truth" figures are the generator's and the service never reads them. No outside model or service is called.
