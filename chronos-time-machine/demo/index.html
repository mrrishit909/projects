10,000 BCE. No cities, no states, no writing.

3000 BCE: the first cities.

Uruk in southern Mesopotamia is the largest settlement on Earth, with about 40,000-45,000 people by the estimates of Morris and Modelski. City sizes on the map are those published estimates (0 figures from Morris, Modelski and Chandler); a hollow ring means the city is listed without a figure.

117 CE: Rome and Han.

Two empires of similar size at either end of the Silk Road. They knew of each other only faintly: one embassy from "Daqin" is recorded at the Han court, in 166 CE.

Names are matched to what they meant at the current year: "Han" in 300 BCE is the Warring State, in 100 CE the dynasty.
