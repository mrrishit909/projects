Election Ops Planner

An invented county's election day at 10:30. Arrival forecasts from past turnout timing, a queue simulation for every polling place, spares moved where they close the most gaps, two check-in devices failing at one site, a dispatch, and a public view that shows wait buckets and nothing else. …
The county, its polling places, precincts, equipment, poll workers and arrivals are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real county or voter. The system counts people in a line; it holds no voter identities and nothing about how anyone votes. No outside model or service is called.
