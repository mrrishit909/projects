Do customer votes decide what gets built?

Data: Atlassian's public Jira (jira.atlassian.com), every suggestion created before 2026-10-01 in Jira, Jira Software, Jira Service Management, Confluence and Bitbucket Cloud. Votes are today's counts.

Summary

  • Votes are concentrated: the top 1% of suggestions hold 43% of all votes, and 40% of suggestions have none.
  • Votes go with building. Among suggestions decided after one to three years, 84% of those with 10–49 votes were built, against 13% with 1–9.
  • The heavily voted ideas are rarely declined: of the 200 most-voted suggestions, 89 were built and 2 declined. But 102 are still open, and the 100 most-voted open ones have waited a median 10.7 years.
  • 15,565 of the 16,178 open suggestions (96%) sit in Gathering Interest. Those closed for low engagement had a median of 3 votes.

Does voting work?

Each line compares suggestions that waited about as long before their decision, because votes pile up while a suggestion waits. Allowing for the wait, product and era, each doubling of votes goes with 2.1 times the odds of being built. That is an association, not proof that votes cause building: a feature Atlassian has already planned can also attract votes.

Recommendations for a product team

  • Report the top of the vote list with its age: a request open for ten years with thousands of votes needs a decision, either way.
  • Close the Gathering Interest pile on a schedule, as Atlassian already does with Low Engagement, and say so to the people who filed them.
  • Don't read zero votes as zero demand: 4,144 suggestions were built with no votes, half of them within 60 days of being filed.

Method

Outcomes come from each suggestion's Jira resolution: built (Fixed, Done, Deployed), declined (Won't Fix, Won't Do), closed for low interest (Timed out, Low Engagement), other (duplicates, answered, invalid ...), or open. Low-interest closures are left out of every votes-and-building comparison, because they are closed for having few votes. Every count was checked against the Jira server's own JQL counts.
