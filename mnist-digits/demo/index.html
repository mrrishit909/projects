Neural network in your browser · MNIST

Draw a digit

Draw one digit, 0-9, in the black square. A small neural network (one hidden layer, 98% accurate on MNIST's 10,000 test images) reads it as you draw. On the right is the 28 × 28 image it actually sees, prepared the way MNIST's digits were.

Draw something

What the model sees: your drawing cropped, scaled into a 20 × 20 box and centred by its centre of mass in a 28 × 28 frame, exactly as MNIST was prepared.

Handwriting from a mouse or finger looks different from 1990s pen on paper, so expect more mistakes than on the test set: thin strokes, very small digits or a crossed 7 often confuse it.

Model: scikit-learn MLP (784 → 128 → 10) trained on MNIST (LeCun, Cortes and Burges; CC BY-SA 3.0); weights stored as 16-bit integers in model.json. Runs entirely in your browser.
