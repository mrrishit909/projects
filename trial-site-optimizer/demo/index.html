Trial Site Optimizer

An invented oncology research network of 120 sites. A phase 3 protocol is parsed into executable criteria by a deterministic grammar and reviewed by a person; the temporal rules engine finds the eligible patients in tokenised records; enrolment and dropout models score every site; a MILP picks 20 sites under budget, a second person approves, and the simulator runs twelve months to check the prediction. …
The network, its patients, protocols and enrolment are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real patient, site or investigator data is used and no outside model or service (no language model) is called. Patient records are stored only as tokens: a keyed hash, an age band, a distance band and events as days before the snapshot.
