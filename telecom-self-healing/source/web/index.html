Network Self-Healing

An invented metro radio network with four weeks of history. A microwave backhaul link degrades and a bad change breaks handovers; hundreds of alarms become two incidents with ranked root causes; the forecast and the digital twin find a reroute inside the policy envelope; a second person approves it, and the evening busy hour stays inside the envelope. …
The network, its traffic, counters and alarms are generated by a simulator in this repository; no operator data is used. The digital twin is the service's own model (forecast traffic over measured capacity); the generator then shows what really happened. No outside model or service is called.
