CITED SCIENCE + SYNTHETIC FOSSILS Clades, dates and extinctions come from published sources (named on every entry). Fossil finds and the drifting continents are simplified stand-ins.

Oceans, then microbes.

The oldest widely accepted fossils are microbial mats about 3.48 billion years old. For most of Earth's history, that was all of life.

Animals, then everything else.

The tree grows as you scroll: each branch starts where its first-appearance range begins, and the faded part of a branch is that range, not a single date.

56 lineages, five mass extinctions.

The worst, the end-Permian, took an estimated 96% of species. The most famous, the K-Pg, ended every dinosaur lineage but one.
