Voyage Fuel Optimizer

An invented container line on the North Atlantic. One ship's crossing from New York to Rotterdam is routed and speeded against the forecast and a berth window; a storm and a full Rotterdam are told to the simulator two days out; the alternatives are compared, a second person approves the change, and the voyage report measures it against the weather-normalised baseline. …
The ocean, its weather and storms, the ships and their reports are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real vessel, AIS or weather data is used. The storm and the port congestion are injected by telling the simulator; the planners only see forecasts, port line-ups and the ships' own reports. No outside model or service is called.
