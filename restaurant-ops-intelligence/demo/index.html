Restaurant Ops Intelligence

An invented 50-store chain on a Sunday night. Monday's lunch rush is forecast store by store, item by item, every 15 minutes; prep and orders follow from the forecast. Then a supplier's truck is late and a rainstorm is coming: the service re-plans, a store manager approves the bigger changes, and the same days are replayed under the old plans to count the waste and stockouts the re-plan saved. …
The chain, its customers, kitchens, suppliers and weather are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real restaurant data is used. Lost sales, spoilage and shrinkage are the generator's truth, which the service never reads. No outside model or service is called.
