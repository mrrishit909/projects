Aircraft Health Ops

An invented fleet of forty aircraft, six months in. One engine's compressor is wearing out: the trend, the anomaly score, remaining life with a band, a hangar night that avoids an AOG, and the mechanic's finding fed back into the reliability record. …
The fleet, its engines, flights, telemetry, fault codes and maintenance history are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real aircraft, engine or sensor, and nothing here is airworthiness advice. Every removal is a proposal a person performs and records. No outside model or service is called.
