Air quality explorer · daily AQI by county · 2015 to 2025

Which days was the air unhealthy?

Pick a county. Each row is a year and each column a day, from 1 January on the left to 31 December on the right. Dull colours are good or moderate days; the bright ones are above 100, unhealthy for sensitive groups or worse; the number at the end of each row counts that year's bright days. A county's value is the highest reading among its monitors.

Move over the grid to read a day

Days dimmed by the filter were set by a different pollutant. An AQI above 500 is beyond the official scale and is shown as "over 500"; a day with no record is left empty. The counties are 24 of the 609 in the project's fixed panel, chosen to cover the big cities and the places the case study mentions.

Data: US EPA AirData, daily AQI by county, files as of 2026-06-25. The page runs in your browser from data.json; nothing is sent anywhere.
