A site is many disciplines, not one opinion.

1,900 parcels in a synthetic port city, each at its zoned height. Scroll: the city rises from a flat grid of lots.

SYNTHETIC DATA The city, owners, rules, comps and prices are generated. No model is called: agents are deterministic analyzers charged simulated tokens.

Pick a parcel and the metro dives to the site.

A surface parking lot on a corner in a mixed-use district. Five agents read their own slices in parallel: parcel facts and mobility on the cheap tier, zoning, market and environment on the mid tier.

Calculators draw the envelope. Agents do not do arithmetic.

The zoning agent turns rule text into cited constraints and says what it cannot resolve; a deterministic calculator extrudes the envelope; the scenario agent proposes programs that must fit inside it; a spreadsheet-parity pro forma gives IRR, NPV and yield on cost. The strongest model sees 5,812 tokens of findings instead of 18,285: 3× less.

  • haiku parcel facts and mobility
  • sonnet zoning, market, environment, scenarios, pro formas
  • opus the investment committee only

A recommendation that challenges its own assumptions.

On the demo parcel the workflow produces 30 findings and 3 feasible programs for $0.65 at list prices. The committee reviews 7 assumptions: 1 unsupported (retail income in a 14% vacancy submarket), 2 weak (no floodproofing cost, optimistic rent growth), re-runs the pro forma with them adjusted, and re-ranks. Nothing is final until a human approves.

Approve the committee recommendation

Approval is the human gate: it records who approved, hashes the exported committee memo and writes the audit log (in the Docker stack). No agent can take this step.
