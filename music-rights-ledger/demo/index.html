Music Rights Ledger

An invented repertoire of 400 works and 586 recordings. Usage files with the usual damage resolved to recordings, a clip matched by landmark fingerprint, royalties split by territory and contract with advances recouped, and an overlapping claim that moves money to hold until a person decides. …
The catalog, its people, companies, usage reports and audio are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real song, writer, label or service appears, and the "audio" is synthetic tone sequences. Royalty pools are stated assumptions. No outside model or service is called.
