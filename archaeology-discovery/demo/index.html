Site Discovery

An invented valley with something buried in it. Two surveys that each show it faintly, fused into candidate areas; one trench; two reconstructions of the same evidence, each with its confidence and every wall's chain of evidence. …
The valley, its surveys, the buried site and the trench are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real place. The hidden truth is shown only to score the methods. No outside model or service is called.
