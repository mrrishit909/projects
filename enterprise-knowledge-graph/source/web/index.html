Enterprise Knowledge Graph

An invented company's HR directory, service catalog, wiki, tickets, CRM and chat, read into one graph with its owners over time. Ask why a vendor was chosen, who owns a failing service, trace the evidence, and see that a person without access to a source cannot get its facts out, through any door. …
The company, its people, documents and permissions are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing real is used. Extraction, resolution and answers are deterministic models in this repository: no language model or outside service is called.
