Farm Crop Intelligence

An invented farm at day 90 of the season. Where is the crop in trouble before the harvest shows it, what will each field yield, and how should a quarter less water be spread? …
The farm, its weather, images, probes and yields are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real field. The soil-water model used for planning is the one that generated the data, which is the best case. Prices and the water cost are stated assumptions. No outside model or service is called.
