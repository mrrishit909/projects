Opponent Intelligence

An invented basketball league. Our next opponent's latest match arrives as a tracking file: name every play, learn their habits with the clock running down, pick the lineup to defend them, and let an analyst correct what the rules got wrong. …
The league, its logs and the tracking record are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real team. There is no video: the tracks are drawn by the generator, not detected. The play rules were written for the generator's signatures, so their accuracy is a statement about the generator. No outside model or service is called.
