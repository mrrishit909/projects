Fab Yield Intelligence

An invented fab with 27 chambers and eight weeks of lots. One etch chamber starts running hot; the drift detector, the wafer-map classifier and root-cause ranking find it; a second person approves the quarantine; and a counterfactual re-run shows what a recipe change would have saved. …
The fab, its lots, sensors and test maps are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real fab data is used. The counterfactual re-runs the same generator, so it is as right as the simulator's physics. No outside model or service is called.
