Warehouse Robot Orchestration

250 robots in an invented warehouse during a peak order wave. Two aisles are blocked, a charger fails, an operator stops a robot in traffic and five urgent orders arrive; every robot is re-planned without a collision, a plan that takes robots off their work waits for a second person, and the same shift is replayed under a FIFO dispatcher. …
The warehouse, its robots, batteries, orders and disruptions are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real warehouse or fleet data is used. The floor executes moves exactly as commanded, and its collision checker is the judge of every plan. No outside model or service is called.
