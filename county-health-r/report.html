What goes with longer lives in US counties

County Health Rankings 2025 · 2,992 counties

Life expectancy across 2,992 counties averages 75.1 years with a standard deviation of 3.6. Seventeen county factors, all measured together, explain 72.2% of that variation; a typical county is predicted within 1.9 years.
