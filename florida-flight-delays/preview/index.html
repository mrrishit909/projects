Reading it: "late-arriving aircraft" means the plane was already late from its previous flight. It is the biggest cause, and it is the same knock-on effect the departure-hour chart shows: the first flights of the day leave on time, and every delay after that rolls forward. Weather causes far fewer delay minutes than most people guess, but it drives most cancellations (59%, mostly January through March).
