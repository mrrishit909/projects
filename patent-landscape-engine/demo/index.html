Patent Landscape Engine

An invented field of 4,000 battery-pack patents and 600 papers. A proposed invention broken into claim elements, prior art retrieved and re-ranked by how many elements each reference covers, an evidence matrix, the closest two-reference combination, competitor clusters and white space, judged against concepts the search never sees. …
The patents, papers, assignees, inventors, citations and the concepts behind them are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real patent or a real company. No language model and no downloaded embedding model is used: the dense space is LSA learned from this corpus. Hidden truth is shown only to judge the search.
