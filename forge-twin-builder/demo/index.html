An empty grid. Scroll, and a production line assembles on it from the asset library: every piece is a real, editable asset with connectors.

SYNTHETIC DATA The plant, its telemetry and every number here come from simulators in this repository.

8 assets, port to port.

Raw material, an infeed conveyor, a CNC mill, a buffer rack, a robot cell, a press, an outfeed conveyor and finished goods. Each one snapped onto the last by its connectors.

Telemetry moves the machines.

The 2 machines publish RPM, temperature, vibration, flow and status. Bound to the model, RPM turns the spindles and temperature colours the housings. Nothing here animates on its own.

Then ask it a question.

Parts flow through a discrete-event simulation of this line. Where does it jam, and what would a second CNC machine buy you? Branch it, simulate it, compare it, with confidence intervals.
