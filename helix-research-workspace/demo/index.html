From the genome: one variant in one gene.

SLC9X4 p.R412Q is a missense change in exon 10 of 14, in the ninth transmembrane helix. Of — papers, 58 mention this disease or variant, and 26 of those are a preprint of a paper already counted. A cheap agent removes them first.

To the protein, and what it does to the drug.

The variant sits in the transporter's ninth helix and cuts its activity to about 40% of wild type. Veltrazine restores heterozygous models but not homozygous ones. A genomics agent and a drug-evidence agent say so, each with the assay or paper behind it.

A pathway of claims, and a critic that looks for the contradiction.

Eight small studies say carriers respond better. One randomized substudy of 640 patients says they do not. The graph grows as agents return; when the critic applies the trial, the association edge turns red. 7 claims, 3 flagged, $0.62 a run at list prices.

Review and export the research brief

Review is the human gate: it records who reviewed, hashes the exported brief and writes the audit log (in the Docker stack). The export carries the research-only disclaimer and the provenance of every claim. No agent can take this step, and nothing here is a clinical recommendation.
